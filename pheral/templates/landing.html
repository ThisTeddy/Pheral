A note from the founder

Paying a friend shouldn't take ten messages.

I built Pheral after too many group chats that went "send account number", "which bank?", "Opay" before a single naira moved. People already talk about money and work in the same place they talk about everything else. Our apps just never caught up.

So Pheral keeps the chat, the payment and the job in one thread, with a receipt for every move. We're early, and what you tell us shapes what we build next.
