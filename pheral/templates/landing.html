What Pheral is

Pheral is a messaging app with a wallet inside it.

You can talk to someone, send them money, or pay them for a job without leaving the conversation. Every payment is recorded.

Features

What is in the app

Messages

Direct chats and group chats, with photos, voice notes and files. You can see when a message has been read.

Payments

Send money to another Pheral user from inside the conversation. They are notified at once, and both of you get a receipt.

Currencies

Keep a wallet in each currency you use and convert between your own wallets. You are shown the amount you will receive, fee included, before you confirm.

Group wallets

A shared balance for a class, a family or a team. Members contribute, everyone can see the contributions, and only an admin can withdraw.

Hiring

Post a job or apply to one, agree the price in the chat, and release payment from your wallet when the work is done.

Bank withdrawals

Move money from your wallet to a Nigerian bank account. The transfer stays marked as pending until the bank confirms it.

How money is handled

Security and records

Money moves according to a few plain rules.

A balance can only be spent once.

A wallet is locked while money is moving, so two requests cannot use the same funds.

Every move is recorded.

Each payment writes a ledger entry. Payments between people also produce a receipt with a reference number.

Cards and bank payments go through Flutterwave.

Card details are handled by the payment provider, not stored by Pheral.

Unconfirmed transfers are held, not guessed at.

If a bank transfer does not return a clear answer, the amount stays on hold until the result is confirmed. It is refunded if the transfer fails.

Why it exists

A note from the founder

ES

Endurance Saviour

Founder, Pheral

Port Harcourt

I built the first version of Pheral between Waterlines and Woji in Port Harcourt, mostly at night. I was tired of watching people move money by asking for an account number, then a bank name, then a screenshot, all inside a chat.

Money and work already happen in our conversations. Pheral keeps the chat, the payment and the job in one place and records each step. It is an early product and it still has rough edges. If something does not work for you, tell us and we will fix it.

FAQ

Common questions

Short answers to the things people ask first.

Is it free?+

Joining and chatting cost nothing. If something does cost money, you see the exact amount before you confirm, never after.

Which currencies are supported?+

NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR, UGX, TZS, RWF, EGP, USD, GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD.

How does paying across currencies work?+

You send in your currency and the other person receives theirs. We show the amount that will arrive, with the fee already included, and hold that rate briefly so it cannot change while you decide.

Can a balance be spent twice?+

No. A wallet is locked while money moves, and every move is written to a ledger. If a bank transfer cannot be confirmed, we wait for the real answer before refunding you.

Do I need to install anything?+

No. Pheral runs in your browser. If you add it to your home screen, it opens full screen like any other app.

Does it work outside Nigeria?+

Wallets and sign-up work in several countries. Paying out to a bank account is available in Nigeria only for now.
